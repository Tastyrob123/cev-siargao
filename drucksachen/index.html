The website, eight tools and twenty-four pieces for print and Instagram, in the order of a day on Tourism Road: lunch from 12 to 3 pm and dinner from 5:30 to 9 pm, Monday to Saturday.

12 PM · At the table

Stands, coaster and the bill

Three inserts for the acrylic stand: the drinks page of the menu, the seven chef’s recommendations marked *** and 4.8 on Google from 1,418 reviews. A coaster in the zigzag rim of the plates, a napkin, head and foot of the receipt, two stamps and a card with the number for the next reservation.

1 PM · On the menu

The menu in print

CEV’s menu on two pages, set like the menu on the tables: appetizers, extras, ceviche and kinilaw on page one, cocktails, dessert, refreshments and beers on page two, in A4 and A3.

3 PM · Around General Luna

For hostels and surf camps

Two flyers, three postcards and a notice with tear-off strips, with the map of Tourism Road and CEV beside Kudo Surf, the address, the opening hours and a QR code. For the road a chalkboard and a banner.

Flyers, board, banner

5:30 PM · Under the sign

Forty stickers

Forty stickers on six sheets: the fish logo, the look of CEV’s Instagram and printed menu, the wall with the three framed letters and the breeze blocks, the house’s own lines and the twelve dishes, each sheet as a print file with cut line and as SVG.

9 PM · On Instagram

The next thirty posts

The next thirty posts in a phone you can scroll: twenty-four photos from the house and six set posts in CEV’s red and white, with David’s line about vinegar, the chef’s recommendations, the main courses with prices, the rating on Google, the map and the thanks after the 2026 tables. Next to them fourteen stories.
