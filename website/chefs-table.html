Island Style Chef’s Tables

Seven tables in 2026 with guest kitchens from Siargao, Manila, Hong Kong and Seattle. Some took place on Mam-on Island, others on the beachfront of Punta Dolores.

A feast on banana leaves

A kamayan, or boodle fight, is informal and generous: grilled seafood, meat fresh off the fire, bright kinilaw and rice piled high, laid out over banana leaves. Everyone stands shoulder to shoulder and eats with their hands.

“It is something truly special, something that promotes a strong sense of family, community and freedom, and something we want more people to experience.”David del Rosario, chef and owner of CEV

“Salamat karajaw sa ijo tanan!”CEV to everyone at the 2026 tables

Table by table

22 March · Mam-on Island

Bourke’s, Hong Kong

Head chef Tara del Rosario, David’s cousin, born in Manila and raised in Hong Kong, came with managing partner Brett Goss. It was their second table together, after a collaboration in Hong Kong the August before.

“Think shrimps on the barbie paired with a passionfruit and sea urchin kinilaw.”

24 guests · 50 minutes by boat from General Luna · two cocktails by Bourke’s, one by CEV, local beer

12 April · Siargao

Roots × Last Chance

Two from the island: the kitchen of Marina and Ines Castañeda and the cocktail bar Last Chance.

24 May · Manila

Linamnam

The third table, with chef Don Baldosano of Linamnam in Manila.

June · Manila

Toyo Eatery

Tatler’s Best Restaurant of the Year 2026 in the Philippines.

30 August · Mam-on Island

Hapag & Ayà, Manila

The community day of the Siargao Food and Wine Festival. The teams of Hapag, a Tatler Best 20 restaurant, and its sister concept Ayà built the boodle around two techniques: kinilaw, raw, and sugba, grilled.

The festival, in its second edition, ran six days of collaborations, workshops and dinners across the island.

“This isn’t just about putting on a great dinner—it’s about opening our home.”

Andre Malarky, festival director · bar by Last Chance

September · Punta Dolores

Musang & Kilig, Seattle

Table six, on the beachfront of Punta Dolores, with the chefs of Musang and Kilig from Seattle.

“What an incredible day. Definitely another one for the books!”

CEV on Instagram · with thanks to Punta Dolores and Galatea

1 November · Punta Dolores

FEATR, Manila

The finale with Erwan Heussaff. The FEATR chefs brought Filipino ingredients they had found travelling the Philippines. Dessert by Galatea came in a floating nipa hut in the mangroves of Secret Beach.

11 am–5 pm · Php 4,800 per person · van transfers from General Luna · paddle boards · CEV cocktails
